The current platform

Electric performance,
engineered by students.

A compact high-voltage powertrain, lightweight chassis, and fully independent suspension - designed to turn learning into motion.

Current vehicle specifications

Motor torque
100 Nm / 73.7 ft-lb
Discharge power
60 kW
Energy capacity
7 kWh
Peak current
270 A
Peak voltage
302.4 V
Vehicle weight
496 lb / 225 kg
Max lateral acceleration
1.4 g

Key systems & materials

  • EMRAX 188 motor + Rinehart Motion Systems PM100DXR controller
  • IM7 8551-7 unidirectional pre-preg carbon fiber + HexPly 914 twill-weave pre-preg
  • HexWeb CR-III 5052 aluminum honeycomb core
  • Fully independent double-wishbone suspension

Specifications reflect the team’s current supplied data and may change as design and testing progress.
